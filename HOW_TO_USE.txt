HOW TO USE: song-era-harmony-analyzer
==================================================

PURPOSE
  Profiles harmonic trends, chord vocabularies, signature 4-chord loops, and
  songwriting habits across musical decades (1960s through 2000s) using 16,800+
  dated songs from the Lakh MIDI dataset on Drive B.

CAPABILITIES
  - Generates decade-specific chord quality distributions (Triads vs 7ths vs Suspended vs Extended)
  - Identifies top recurring 4-chord loops that defined each specific era
  - Cites real famous songs from the era in the database (Prince, Sting, Soundgarden, ZZ Top)
  - Delivers period-accurate songwriting recipes for writing authentic retro music
  - Compares harmonic complexity evolution across decades (e.g. 60s folk/blues vs 80s pop vs 90s alt)
  - Seamlessly bridges to ableton-midi-chord-builder for 1-click MIDI progression clip export

USE CASES
  - Understand what chord habits made 80s synth-pop sound distinct from 90s alternative
  - Discover the most popular chord loops of the 1970s funk/rock era
  - Write a new track with authentic vintage harmonic structures and period voicings
  - Analyze how dominant 7th chord usage transitioned into minor 7th and power triad loops
  - Find real-world song references from a specific release year

IDEAS
  - Chain with ableton-midi-chord-builder to generate authentic 80s synth pads or 70s Rhodes MIDI clips
  - Pair with reharmonization-engine to take a 60s triad loop and modernize it into 90s neo-soul
  - Analyze the decade transition between 1980s synth-pop and 1990s grunge harmonic patterns
  - Generate genre-accurate backing track chord templates for period film scoring and game design
  - Create decade-themed songwriting challenges with authentic harmonic constraints

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  What chords made 80s synth-pop sound the way it did?

  What were the most popular chord progressions of the 1970s?

  Give me authentic, period-accurate chord loops for a 90s R&B or grunge track.

  How did chord complexity change between the 60s and 2000s?

  Show me chord habits and famous tracks from the 1960s in the database.

EXAMPLE FLOW
  You:   What chords made 80s synth-pop sound so distinct?
  Model: [Analyzes 2,435 songs and 206,000 chords from the 1980s]
         - Major triads: 48.5%, Minor triads: 17.0%, Minor 7ths: 11.7%
         - Signature trait: Heavy use of suspended (sus4) and added 9th chords
         - Top 4-chord loop: C -> F -> G -> C (and G -> C -> D -> G)
         - Iconic examples: Daniel Balavoine (1985), Sting (1987), ZZ Top (1983)
         - Recipe: Avoid jazz 7ths on dominant; use open sus4 and bVI-bVII-i cadences.
         [Offers 1-click Ableton MIDI clip generation via ableton-midi-chord-builder]

DEFAULT GOALS IF YOU DON'T NAME ONE
  1. Determine the target decade or era from your prompt (defaulting to the 1980s if unspecified).
  2. Query `era_harmony.py` across the 16,800+ dated song dataset on Drive B.
  3. Break down the chord quality percentages (triads vs 7ths vs suspended vs extended).
  4. Present the top 3–4 recurring 4-chord loops of that decade.
  5. Cite 3 iconic tracks with release years and chord progressions.
  6. Provide an authentic, period-accurate songwriting recipe with optional Ableton MIDI export.
